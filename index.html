What do I need to set up?+

Save your home address in Settings, then allow Always location access, Precise Location, and notifications. Add any friends’ places you want Polarys to recognize.

Location access helps the app recognize visits and departures. Notifications let it send the ride reminder. You can manage permissions in your iPhone’s Settings.

Read the privacy policy
When will I get a reminder?+

Polarys looks for visits to bars and saved party addresses between 9 in the evening and 5 in the morning, in your local time. After at least 45 minutes at these places, detecting your departure can trigger a reminder.

Reminders depend on your permissions, GPS, and iOS. They can arrive late or fail to arrive. Make a plan before you leave, and open the Ride tab whenever you need it.

Does it tell me if I’m sober?+

No. The activity level on the home screen is based on venue time and a qualifying connection to a saved car. It does not measure alcohol or tell you whether you can drive.

If you say you were planning to drive, the app asks how much you’ve had to drink. Your answer isn’t saved, and every answer leads to ride options.

What happens if I connect to my car?+

You can save your car’s Bluetooth connection in Settings. After time at a bar, a connection to that car can prompt Polarys to encourage another way home.

Keep Polarys open for car detection. This is another reminder to consider a ride, not a way to measure sobriety.

How do I get the app?+

Polarys is currently an iPhone beta. Open the beta link on your iPhone, install Apple’s TestFlight if prompted, then accept the invitation to install Polarys.

The person behind Polarys

Hi, I’m Anay.

I’m an Iowa State student building Polarys. The idea is pretty simple: getting home should be part of the plan, even when the rest of the night wasn’t planned at all.

I named it after the North Star. I liked the idea of something that helps you find your way home. I’m testing the iPhone app with friends and working through what’s useful on an actual night out.
